# Witchr Sanctum — Private Test Round 1 Debrief Script

This debrief script is designed for the session organizer to evaluate 3–5 independent, unguided testers navigating the Witchr Sanctum MVP (`/sanctum`).

> **PROTOCOL REMINDER:**
> Do NOT force testers to answer questions during the experience. Allow the tester to explore and interact naturally without interruption. Administer this debrief immediately following their unassisted session.

---

## 1. Understanding
1. In your own words, what do you think Sanctum is?
2. What did you expect to happen when you entered?
3. Was anything unclear before you clicked your first option?

## 2. First Choice
4. Which feature did you try first?
5. Why did you pick that one?

## 3. Daily Tarot
6. Did the Daily Tarot draw feel meaningful or generic?
7. Was the interpretation too long, too short, or about right?
8. Would you realistically come back tomorrow for another card?

## 4. Three-Card Reading
9. Did the Three-Card Reading help you think about your question differently?
10. Did the Oracle's combined interpretation feel connected to the three cards?
11. Did anything feel repetitive or overly vague?

## 5. Create a Working
12. Did the Working feel like something you could actually do?
13. Did it make sense why each ingredient was selected?
14. Did the Working feel practical, interesting, strange, confusing, or useful?
15. Was there something you expected to be able to enter that you couldn't?

## 6. Grimoire
16. Did you understand what saving to the Grimoire meant?
17. Did you save anything?
18. If you did not save anything, why not?
19. Would you care about keeping a history of readings and Workings?

## 7. Streaks & Sacred Marks
20. Did you notice the Sanctum streak?
21. Did you notice the Sacred Marks / badges?
22. Did either make you more interested in returning?
23. Did the rewards feel motivating or unnecessary?

## 8. Overall Impression
24. What was your favorite part?
25. What was the weakest part?
26. What confused you?
27. What felt unnecessary?
28. What did you expect Sanctum to do that it currently doesn't?
29. Would you use this again?
30. If yes, what would bring you back most often?

---

## Session Organizer Notes & Prompt Copying
Testers can also open the **"Tell Us What Felt Off"** dialog at the top of any Sanctum chamber to copy these core prompts directly to their clipboard if submitting written feedback asynchronously.
